Hello, I’m Bingshen.

I’m an environmental engineering graduate and an MSc student in Tropical Hydrogeology and Environmental Engineering at Technische Universität Darmstadt , Germany.

My background brings together GIS and remote sensing, computational methods, and hands-on environmental data collection. I’m interested in how these tools can help us understand the environments we live in.

Previously, I studied environmental engineering at Óbuda University in Hungary and gained internship experience in laboratory analysis and data management.
